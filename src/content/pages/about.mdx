## Our Mission

We're building tools that help teams ship better software, faster.

## The Team

Narua was founded in 2024 by a small team of engineers and designers who were tired of the complexity of existing tools. We believe software should be simple, reliable, and a joy to use.

## Our Values

**Simplicity over cleverness.** We choose the simple solution even when a clever one exists.

**Build for reliability.** Every feature we ship, we ship with confidence.

**Users first.** Every decision starts with: what's best for our users?
