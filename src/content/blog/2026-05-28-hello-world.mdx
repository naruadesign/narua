We've been heads-down building for the past year, and today we're ready to share what we've made.

## What is Narua?

Narua is a platform that helps software teams ship better products with less friction. We handle the infrastructure, integrations, and operational complexity so your team can focus on the product.

## Why we built it

Every team we talked to had the same problem: too much time spent on tooling, not enough time building features. We set out to fix that.
